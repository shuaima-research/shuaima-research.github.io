I am an aspiring PhD researcher with interests in **urban development, housing policy, and the application of AI and machine learning to climate-resilient real estate and urban systems**.

My research focuses on how data-driven and interpretable machine-learning models can support more equitable and climate-resilient urban planning and investment decisions.

## Research Interests

- **Climate-Resilient Urban Development** — urban spatial structure, climate adaptation, infrastructure investment
- **Housing Affordability & Real Estate Markets** — supply constraints, zoning, climate risk pricing, policy evaluation
- **Interpretable ML for Policy Analysis** — equity-aware modeling, fairness constraints, decision rules
- **Urban Data Science & Spatial Analytics** — spatial prediction, feature engineering from built-environment data
